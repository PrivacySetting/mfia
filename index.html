How to get in

Four steps. Five minutes.

Join the Discord

Everything runs through it: applications, dues, conflicts, sells.

Press Apply

In the Discord, or here on the site. Two short forms: name, UID, age, Steam hours, when you play, your experience and why you. Clips help. Current members apply too.

Get accepted

Accepted now means you are in for the next wipe. When it opens you get Associate and the [MFIA] tag, and the Don adds you in game from your UID.

Pay your wipe dues

Hand the Don liquid cash in game inside your first 24 hours. That makes you a Soldato. From there the bot promotes you on hours and fights.

How you move up

  • Higher rank means more trust and more time put in. Nothing else.
  • The bot counts your hours in voice with the family and the conflicts you fight in.
  • Up to Made Man it promotes you the moment you hit the numbers.
  • Goodfella needs the Don's yes. Above that, the Don picks.
  • Ranks reset every wipe. Come back and you climb faster.

What costs you rank

  • A strike blocks your next promotion.
  • Two strikes drops you a rank. Three is a kick.
  • You get a strike for missing a conflict you signed up for, or sitting in another voice room during one.
  • Miss five conflicts in a row without a word and that is a strike too. Showing up clears the count.

Earner

A gold tag on top of your rank, for giving well past the dues. Earners sell before everyone else at their rank.

Voice rooms

  • ❓ Help Desk: stuck on anything.
  • 🍷 Piazza Lounge: hang out.
  • 🎲 Trio 1 to 8: three seats each, for grinding.
  • ⚔️ Department of War: gather here before every conflict and sell.
  • ⚔️ Bank · Hotel · Strip Club · Gas Station: one room per fight.

Land and settlements

  • Your base sits on a plot inside a settlement that a player owns.
  • Plot title: $100K (level 1), $800K (level 2), $2M (level 3). It must match the district level.
  • Settlement title: $200K, $2M, $6M.
  • The settlement owner takes 5% of the cash residents launder.
  • Living in a higher district unlocks more Black Market items and Shipments.

Base defense and raids

  • Fuses add defense: +1 uncommon, +3 epic, +5 legendary.
  • Raiders need more decoders for every fuse in the box.
  • A breach opens every door, gate and window for a while.
  • Raid block lasts 7 minutes. Another breach adds 3, never past 7.
  • Repairing a breached box takes 7 seconds. Fuses are not destroyed.
  • Fit an alarm. The epic one warns only the owner.

Police and wanted level

  • You hold up to 3 wanted points. Each civilian killed adds one.
  • At 3 you are Most Wanted and the whole city sees where you are.
  • Cuffing takes 2.5 seconds. The holding cell is 90 seconds.
  • Bribes go up in $10,000 steps, to $200,000 at most.
  • Arresting a wanted criminal pays the officer $15,000.

The four big heists

  • Bank gold vault: needs a Drill. 7 piles of 9 gold bars, about $52,000 a bar.
  • Police armory: needs an ID Card, bartered from 20 Evidence.
  • Grand Hotel exhibition car: needs a Jammer.
  • Gallery exhibit: the fourth city-wide job.
  • All four alert everyone in Looty Bay. Bring the family.

Selling loot

  • Fences are marked with a $ on the map. Six in the city.
  • City sellers charge three times the base price. Buy from the Black Market when you can.
  • In the family, the highest rank sells first.

Clans and claims

  • Businesses are held by clans, not players. Each one has a claim board at its entrance.
  • Districts add laundering speed for the whole clan and pay tax.
  • Taxes are collected in the settlement where the clan leader lives. Protect it.

FAQ

I am already a member. Do I apply?

Yes. Ranks reset when the wipe opens Friday. Everyone goes through the new form.

Where do I find my UID?

It is your Rob Run player ID. Put it in the Apply form or in 🆔│drop-uid. The Don uses it to add you to the clan in game. If you cannot find it, ask in Help Desk voice.

I was accepted but I am not in the clan in game.

The Don adds people by hand from their UID. Check that your UID is dropped and correct, then wait. Do not ping.

Can I pay dues with a clan transfer?

No. Liquid cash, handed to the Don in game. Clan transfers do not count.

What if I miss the 24 hours?

The bot warns you and your name goes on the Don's list. People who do not pay and do not show up get cut when space is needed.

Do I need a mic?

Not to join. For conflicts you need to hear the calls, so be in voice.

How do I get promoted?

Pay dues, be in voice, show up to conflicts. The bot tracks it. Leaders promote from the numbers.

I got scammed in the black market.

Report it to a leader with proof. Scamming or lying is an automatic kick.

I was kicked. Now what?

One message in ⚖️│appeals. Leadership decides. The decision is final.
